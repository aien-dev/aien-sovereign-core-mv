# AIEN Open Cooperation Covenant 1.0

A Voluntary Bilateral Agreement for Global Human Progress and Shared AI Research.

---

## Voluntary Notice

Participation in this Covenant is completely voluntary.

Downstream utilization, reproduction, distribution, compilation, or deployment of AIEN software under the Apache License, Version 2.0 does NOT require signing, ratifying, or assenting to this Covenant. This document establishes an opt-in partnership framework for research laboratories, enterprises, academic institutions, and independent developers committed to mutual research transparency and reciprocal advancement.

---

## Preamble

Artificial intelligence is an intellectual milestone of human civilization. True technological progress requires transparency, mutual trust, and global cooperation across national and institutional boundaries. 

When organizations operate in isolation, conceal hardware bottlenecks, and accelerate competitive races without coordination, the human species faces unnecessary risks. The AIEN Open Cooperation Covenant establishes an open framework for willing partners to share compute insights, demystify hardware scaling, publish open model weights, and guarantee research freedoms for developers worldwide.

---

## Article I: The One Team Commitment

Signatories to this Covenant affirm that artificial intelligence should be developed with shared transparency for the betterment of humanity:

1. **Mutual Respect**: Signatories acknowledge the legitimate contributions of researchers and engineers globally, actively working across geopolitical and institutional divides.
2. **Collaborative Assistance**: Signatories agree to treat compute bottlenecks, memory limitations, and energy scaling as shared engineering challenges requiring open technical exchange.
3. **Upstream Contribution**: Signatories agree that general runtime optimizations, C-ABI bridges, and hardware kernels engineered for open runtimes are contributed back to public upstream repositories.

---

## Article II: Reciprocal Model Weight Transparency

1. **Shared Foundation Weights**: Signatories with annual capitalization or revenues exceeding twenty-five million United States Dollars ($25,000,000 USD) that utilize cooperative engineering or pooled compute resources under this program to train or optimize foundation models agree to publish resulting model weights, architecture specifications, and inference recipes openly to humanity under open-weight or open-source terms within thirty (30) days of general production deployment.
2. **Safe Harbor**: Independent researchers, students, educational institutions, non-profit entities, and startups operate under unconditional safe harbor with zero obligation for mandatory weight release.

---

## Article III: Reciprocal Model Distillation and Evaluation Rights

1. **Unencumbered Research Distillation**: A Signatory providing paid commercial artificial intelligence services or inference APIs agrees that other Signatories and legitimate users paying published commercial fees may utilize resulting model outputs for scientific research, benchmarking, evaluation, fine-tuning, and student-model knowledge distillation.
2. **Elimination of Artificial Distillation Bans**: Signatories agree not to impose restrictive terms of service, arbitrary account suspensions, or non-compete clauses against paying users who distill model outputs into open-source or local architectures. Open distillation is recognized as essential for algorithmic efficiency, safety evaluation, and global technological empowerment.

---

## Article IV: Hardware and Compute Cooperation

1. **Heterogeneous Compute Support**: Signatories collaborate to expand inference efficiency across commodity workstations, edge silicon, and accelerator clusters.
2. **Transparent Operational Sharing**: When compute bottlenecks arise, Signatories commit to open technical post-mortems and shared algorithmic ablations to accelerate community-wide solutions.
