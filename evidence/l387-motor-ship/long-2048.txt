Versioned chunk identity  ### 1.1 Units  - A **chunk** is `chunk_tokens = chunk_blocks * block_size` consecutive positions of one sequence, aligned to a multiple of `chunk_tokens`. `chunk_blocks` is a setting fixed into the namespace (default 16 blocks; with the 16-token blocks of `model_dir.rs` that is 256 tokens (calc:ceaa1589), LMCache's default). A chunk is **full** when it holds `chunk_tokens` positions. The last chunk of a parked sequence may be **partial** (`token_count < chunk_tokens`); partial chunks are keyed with their exact token count and therefore only ever match an identical prefix of identical length. Only full chunks are prefix-share candidates. - Hash everywhere is SHA-256 (host: `sha2`, already in the lockfile; native: argus `sha256.h`, used by `store_v1.h`). blake3 is not used because native has no blake3.  ### 1.2 Namespace id (binds everything that must be equal for bytes to be reusable)  `namespace_id = SHA-256( "AIEN-PKV-NS-V2\0" || fields )` (V1 until sc#504;
