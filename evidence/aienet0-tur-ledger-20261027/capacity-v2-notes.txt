CAPACITY-DISCHARGE CORRECTION: HOST_TESTED ON THE DGX SPARK

Finding and cause
The shared wcmd append precheck required SKEYS + 4 <= KCAP for every new
mutation, including settle/refund. Those operations touch only the reserved job
and its existing account IDs; they allocate no keys. Near capacity, legitimate
escrow could therefore become impossible to discharge. The defect is present in
the authenticated source at Core #794's initial commit
6a0bfc0bb0b08d6fa08bc8109536218f50dd8b2c, not just merged #792.

Fix
Remove the blanket append guard. vpre counts its new PID plus a missing requester;
vres counts its new job plus missing distinct provider/verifier accounts, with
an existing requester required. Refuse 14 before append if the exact slots do
not fit. Settlement/refund remain allocation-free, including at a full table.
No signature, ownership, conservation, single-writer or fsync gate is weakened.
No journal-format or authorization-message change from the earlier v2 commit.

    before=$(sha256sum "$D/journal")
