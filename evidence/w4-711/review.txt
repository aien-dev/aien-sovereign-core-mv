Arithmetic bound review
Finite E4M3FN magnitude decoded in units of 1/512 is at most 229376.
Multiplication by a finite BF16 mantissa at most 255 yields raw <=58490880,
strictly below 2^26. Thus base power <=25 and raised power <=26.
Rounding midpoint and scaled raw comparisons fit well within unsigned 64 bits.
For base power p, raw lies below 2^(p+1), so reconstruction target raw*8
lies below 16*2^p. The final grid point is 12*2^p. Saturation error is
less than 4*2^p; unsaturated nearest error is at most 2*2^p.
At p<=25 this makes each error less than 2^27.
For the raised trial, original maximum is below 8*2^p, so its nearest error
is at most 2^p with p<=26, also below 2^27.
Each squared error is below 2^54; 32 terms total below 2^59. No overflow in
the 64-bit sum or products. Scale arithmetic is bounded before output.
